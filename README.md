# Compensation

A place for contributors to the Monocle open-source project to submit compensation requests for completed work.

## Context

Monocle Network relies on dedicated contributors who give their time, expertise, and resources to operate an effective Haveno network. When the platform generates sufficient revenue through trade fees, contributors receive compensation.

## Goals

The goal of this repository is to provide an efficient, clear framework for contributors to get compensated for their work. 

This repository helps us achieve the following:
* **Increase transparency** regarding project fund allocation.
* **Decentralize the management** and validation of contribution compensation.
* **Compensate contributors directly in Monero (XMR)** to uphold our privacy values.

# Role Compensation

## Compensation Rates

Monocle uses flat-rate structures for our defined project roles.

### User-Facing Roles
* **Community Stewards:** $500 USD per month *(minimum 10 hours of active support per week required)*.
* **Support Contributors:** $400 USD per month *(minimum 10 hours of active support per week required)*.
* **Arbitrators:** $20 USD per arbitration case *(expected ticket response time within 24 hours)*.

### Network-Facing Roles
* **Monero Node Operators:** $50 USD per node, per month 
* **Seed Node Operators:** $50 USD per node, per month.
* **Price Node Operators:** $50 USD per node, per month.
* **Markets Operator:** $50 USD per month.

> Operators are expected to run multiple nodes.

### Application & Contributor-Facing Roles
* **Open-Source Application Maintainers:** $500 USD per month.
* **Contributor Hostess** $500 USD per month. *(minimum 10 hours of active community mangement per week required)*.

## Compensation Schedule & Process

Monocle believes that while users of the network deserve strict transaction privacy, they also deserve complete transparency regarding how the network operates. All compensation to contributors is published openly on this GitHub repository for public audit.

### Required Invoice Information
When requesting compensation contributors must open a GitHub issue containing:
1. **GitHub Username:** This must match the handle you use on our other platforms (SimpleX, Matrix) so peers can verify your identity and work history.
2. **Role Performed:** The specific role fulfilled (e.g., support contributor, Monero node operator).
3. **USD Amount:** The flat-rate amount for your role.
4. **Monero Address:** The XMR address where you wish to receive funds.

### Review & Consensus
Once a compensation request is opened, contributors or community members have a window to add positive feedback, request clarifications, or react with a thumbs up/down. All active contributors are encouraged to engage with and validate the requests of peers they work alongside. 

Once the community reaches a clear consensus, the Contributor Hostess will process the transaction. 

### Final Verification
To maintain accountability, the Contributor Hostess will finalize the request by replying with the **Transaction ID (TxID)** and the **Tx Secret Key**. This allows anyone to crypto-graphically confirm that the compensation was successfully sent and received. Contributors must accept this public transparency workflow prior to undertaking any compensated tasks.

### Monthly Timeline
Compensation is handled on a monthly cycle. Compensation is processed at the end of the following month for the work completed during the previous month.

| Days of the Month | Milestone Action Required |
| :--- | :--- |
| **1st – 10th** | Contributors post their role reports and contribution compensation requests. |
| **11th – 20th** | Contributors review, comment on, and validate each other's work. |
| **11th – End of Month** | Contributor Hostess processes finalized compensation. |

# New Contributor Guidelines

Welcome to Monocle! Below are our core operational expectations, core values, and on-boarding pipelines.

## 🔍 Proof of Work Required

All compensated positions require a demonstrated history of contribution. Monocle does not hire individuals without a visible track record. Monocle prioritizes active community members who are already voluntarily assisting others in our ecosystem.

If you are interested in a compensated role, start by supporting the broader Monero ecosystem. Your proof of work does not need to be exclusive to Monocle; valid examples include:
* **Other Open-Source Projects:** Active, verifiable development or management in privacy tech.
* **The Monero Subreddit:** Regularly providing high-quality answers and engaging with users on Reddit.
* **The Haveno Matrix Room:** Actively troubleshooting and assisting peers in our primary chat rooms.

Monocle builds software that puts users first. If you proactively help the Monero community, you are exactly the type of person Monocle wants contributing.
## ⏳ First-Month Trial Period

Prospective contributors seeking ongoing compensated roles must complete a **one-month uncompensated trial period**.

* **On-boarding & Support:** Our team will actively assist, train, and guide you through your new workflow.
* **Core Duties:** You will perform standard role tasks (e.g., handling support tickets or running test nodes), but these initial on-boarding contributions are not eligible for financial compensation.
* **Mutual Evaluation:** This period gives you a risk-free environment to learn the workflow and ensures the role is a healthy, long-term fit for both parties.

> **Note:** Upon successful completion of the trial period and mutual agreement, you will progress to a permanent, paid role.

## 🎭 Nyms Welcome

Monocle is building an network operated entirely by pseudonymous entities (nyms) while maintaining community trust.

* **Strict Anonymity:** We expect all contributors to operate under pseudonyms.
* **No KYC Collection:** We value your privacy and do not collect any personal identity information.
* **Required Data:** We only require your active nym handle and a Monero (XMR) address to issue compensation.

If your pseudonym has a proven track record in the Monero space and you share our core architectural values, reach out to us—we want to earn the community's trust alongside you.
